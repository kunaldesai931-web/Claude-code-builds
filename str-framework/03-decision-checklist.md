# Deliverable 4 — One-Page Pre-Buy Decision Checklist

**The property analogue to a multibagger pre-buy screen. A single property must clear EVERY gate below before capital is deployed. Any one hard FAIL = do not buy. "Verify" items must be resolved to a PASS in writing before exchange.**

---

### Gate 1 — Ownership is durable (Legal/Structural)
- [ ] Interest is **freehold or freehold-equivalent** (Torrens / DLD freehold / PT-PMA-HGB / hotel-licensed condo-freehold). **A bare ≤30-yr leasehold is a FAIL** for a 15–25-yr hold (wasting asset; Phuket auto-renewals are legally void post-2025).
- [ ] **No nominee structure** anywhere in the chain (criminalised in Bali and Thailand).
- [ ] If Gold Coast: title held as **joint tenants** with the citizen spouse, FIRB-exemption-for-investment-purpose **confirmed in writing** by a QLD lawyer. *(Verify)*
- [ ] If Phuket condo: **foreign-quota headroom confirmed** in the building, with a view to resale liquidity.

### Gate 2 — The rental model is legal (STR Operating)
- [ ] **Daily STR is legal** at this specific address (Phuket → hotel-licensed building ONLY; Bali → PT PMA + KBLI 55193 + zoning + valid NIB).
- [ ] **Body-corporate / strata / building NOC permits** short-term letting (Dubai developer NOC; QLD cannot ban; check by-laws elsewhere).
- [ ] Management is **light-oversight / full-service or branded** (fits the annual-visit mandate) at a **realistic fee assumed at the top of the 15–35% range**.

### Gate 3 — The yield is real (net of everything)
- [ ] **Net post-tax INR yield ≥ 3.5%**, computed as: gross − management − full opex stack (strata/service charge, utilities, cleaning, platform, marketing, insurance, capex reserve) − source tax − **Indian tax top-up**. Headline gross is irrelevant.
- [ ] Occupancy underwritten **conservatively** (Dubai ≤60%, Bali/Phuket ≤65% blended, Gold Coast at citywide STR baseline) and **cross-checked against paid AirDNA data**, not broker claims.
- [ ] Cheque size **above the minimum-viable** for the market (Bali ≥$350k · Phuket ≥$250k · Gold Coast ≥$450k · Dubai ≥$350k).

### Gate 4 — The Indian tax overlay is modelled, not assumed
- [ ] All-in tax leakage computed **as % of gross rent AND as % of capital gain on exit** (no "tax-free" claim accepted — Dubai pays **full Indian tax, zero FTC**).
- [ ] **FTC failure modes checked:** zero-source-tax (Dubai), gross-vs-net mismatch leakage, **Form 67 filing scheduled** for every relevant year.
- [ ] **Exit modelled in rupees** (no foreign-currency gain protection for residents; no indexation; 12.5% LTCG + surcharge).
- [ ] **Surcharge-cliff plan exists:** realisations staggered across financial years so a lumpy gain does not surcharge the whole base income.
- [ ] Gold Coast only: **non-resident CGT (no 50% discount) + 15% FRCGW gross withholding** explicitly built into the exit IRR. *(This gate fails most Gold Coast yield cases — buy for appreciation, not yield.)*

### Gate 5 — The cycle and the FX are respected
- [ ] **Not buying into the forecast 2026 peak with the full envelope** — entry phased; largest tranche reserved for the cycle low.
- [ ] No late-cycle red flags on *this* asset (off-plan-above-ready pricing, developer "guaranteed yield," marketing-led demand).
- [ ] FX tailwind to INR assessed on its **own** merit (Dubai cleanest ~3.4%/yr; **Bali IDR least reliable**) — and **not** double-counted as alpha that's actually just USD/INR drift available via US assets.

### Gate 6 — Compliance & repatriation are pre-cleared
- [ ] **LRS staging plan** drawn up (USD 250k/yr/person; multi-year for cheques above that; OCI spouse is a non-resident with **no LRS limit to "share"**).
- [ ] **20% TCS cash-flow lock** (~₹40L per $250k tranche, refundable in 12–18 mo) budgeted.
- [ ] **Schedule FA reporting** committed for every year held (immovable property reported **regardless of value**; Black Money Act = **₹10L/yr penalty** for non-disclosure even if tax fully paid).
- [ ] Repatriation path understood (resident faces **no inbound cap**; FX spread/wire friction budgeted at 0.5–2%/leg).

### Gate 7 — Exit discipline is written down (Pillar-Break)
- [ ] **3–5 pre-committed exit criteria** recorded for this asset *before* purchase (occupancy floor for 2 consecutive years; net post-tax INR yield <3%; STR ban/restriction; source-FX −15% over 18 mo sustained; spouse-residency change for Gold Coast).
- [ ] Annual review scheduled against actuals.

### Gate 8 — It beats the liquid alternative
- [ ] **Base-case 10-yr after-tax INR IRR ≥ 12%**, OR a documented, explicit reason the asset earns its place below the equity hurdle (diversification away from expensive Indian smid-caps, FX, lifestyle/visa optionality) that you would defend in writing.
- [ ] You have asked the two-line check honestly: ***Is this analysis testing the thesis or supporting it? Do the tax, regulatory and STR sources survive a quality-tier and citation-chain check?***

---

**Decision rule:** All eight gates PASS (and all "Verify" items resolved in writing) → deploy the planned tranche. Any hard FAIL → do not buy; if Gate 8 fails with no documented override → the capital belongs in Indian large-cap equity, not this property.
